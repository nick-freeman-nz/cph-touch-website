Summer Sessions Are Back!

The days are getting longer and that means one thing — Wednesday sessions are back! Starting this April, we'll be running our summer schedule with sessions on both Sundays (10:30, all year round) and Wednesdays (17:30, summer time) in Fælledparken. Whether you're a seasoned player or picking up a ball for the first time, everyone's welcome.

Touchtober 2025: What a Weekend!

CPH Touch travelled to Sweden for Touchtober 2025 and what a tournament it was. Over 20 teams from across Scandinavia battled it out in Malmö. Our mixed team made it to the quarter-finals and the social afterwards was legendary. Already counting down to next year!

Est. 2011 Club History

CPH Touch: How It All Started

It started with a few mates in Fælledparken with a ball and a dream. Back in 2011, touch rugby in Denmark was virtually non-existent. A small group of expats and locals decided to change that. From those first Sunday sessions with 6 players, CPH Touch has grown into one of Scandinavia's most active touch rugby communities with 55+ regular players.
